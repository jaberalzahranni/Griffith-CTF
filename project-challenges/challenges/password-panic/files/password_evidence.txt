          SOLD OUT — CYBER INCIDENT INVESTIGATION
                 EVIDENCE FILE #001
========================================================

INCIDENT:
The Starlight Arena surprise concert presale SOLD OUT
in only 90 seconds.

Thousands of genuine fans were unable to purchase tickets.
The Cyber Response Team believes an automated ticket-buying
bot may have gained access to a staff account connected to
the presale system.

--------------------------------------------------------
ACCOUNT UNDER INVESTIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------

Username: presale_admin
Employee: Alex Carter
Role: Ticket Presale Administrator

Investigators recovered an old password reminder belonging
to this account:

        "Favourite band + concert year"

Additional information recovered from the staff profile:

Favourite band: Starlight
Concert year: 2026

--------------------------------------------------------
SECURITY TEAM NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------

Weak passwords are often created using predictable
information such as names, favourite things, dates,
or simple combinations of these.

Your mission:

Determine the weak password that an attacker could have
guessed to access the presale administrator account.
